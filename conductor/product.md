# Product Definition: Emma G Adventures

Educational mobile app for 1st graders (6-year-olds) featuring lessons and quizzes in Math, Reading, Science, and World topics.

## Core Goal
Provide high-quality, visually engaging educational content using a Studio Ghibli-inspired hand-painted aesthetic.

## Target Audience
- 6-year-olds (Early Elementary).
- Requires simple text (max 12 words per slide).
- Heavy reliance on visual learning.
